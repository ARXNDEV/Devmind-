# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent; ask before judging.
- Focus feedback on the code and the problem, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially.
